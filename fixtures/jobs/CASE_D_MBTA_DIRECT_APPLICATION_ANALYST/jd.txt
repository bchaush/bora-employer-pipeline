Application Analyst (Digital Workplace)
Massachusetts Bay Transportation Authority (MBTA)
Job Number: 26-20235
10 Park Plaza, Boston

Employment Type: Full-Time

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

Bachelor's degree from an accredited institution.

Three (3) years of experience in system analysis, including enterprise
application design, configuration / development, implementation, and
support.

Substitutions:
A High School Diploma or GED with an additional seven (7) years of directly
related experience substitutes for the Bachelor's degree requirement.
An Associate's degree from an accredited institution an additional three (3)
years of directly related experience substitutes for the Bachelor's degree
requirement.
A Master's degree in a related subject substitute for two (2) years of
general experience.
A nationally recognized certification, or statewide/professional
certification in a related field substitutes for one year of experience.

REQUIRED SKILLS

Proven ability to map and document complex business processes and identify
opportunities for optimization or automation within assigned applications.

Experience identifying and documenting business rules and technical
requirements.

Foundational knowledge of cloud-based application delivery models such as
Software as a Service (SaaS) and / or technology infrastructure concepts.

Experience with IT service management / ticketing systems.

Strong Microsoft Office proficiency (Word, Excel, Outlook, PowerPoint,
Teams).

Ability to commute to assigned work locations in the Boston, MA metro area,
as required by the role.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

Direct experience with Comply365, Document Management Systems, HR, LMS, IDP.

Power Platform Proficiency: Direct experience developing, governing, or
administering solutions using the Microsoft Power Platform.

API management and data integration tools.

Microsoft 365 governance and security knowledge.

Power BI or Tableau reporting experience.

Public transit/transportation industry background.

WORK AUTHORIZATION

All employees must be legally authorized to work in the United States and
on an unrestricted basis. The MBTA does not have an employer work
sponsorship program.
