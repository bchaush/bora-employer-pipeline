Role Overview
As an Implementation Analyst at Atominvest, you will get hands-on with the
data, systems, and workflows of some of the world's most sophisticated
alternative investment managers, working alongside the rest of the Customer
Success team to bring new clients onto the platform.

You'll work directly with real fund structures, real investor data, and
real operational challenges from day one -- a rare opportunity to build
genuine private markets expertise while still early in your career, rather
than learning the industry from the sidelines. This is a genuinely
technical, detail-driven role -- you'll be in the data, in the
configuration, and in the QA testing, not one step removed from it.

It's also the natural starting point for a career in implementation: as you
build fluency in private markets workflows, platform configuration, and
client delivery, this role is designed to grow into more senior
implementation and client-facing positions, including Implementation
Manager.

What You'll Be Doing
- Get hands-on with customer data, requirements, and workflows -- configuring and implementing them directly within the Atominvest platform
- Work alongside Implementation Managers to onboard customers onto the platform, supporting everything from data migration to UAT
- Analyse real client datasets and fund structures -- gaining direct exposure to how leading private markets firms structure funds, manage investor relationships, and run their operations
- Drive down time to value by ensuring a smooth, well-documented transition to ongoing customer lifecycle management
- Liaise with Product and Engineering to complete extensive QA testing for new product features and bug fixes, and to help scope and troubleshoot integration or data issues
- Field support tickets, ensuring customers and investors are kept up to date on progress toward resolution
- Maintain technical documentation and internal knowledge base
