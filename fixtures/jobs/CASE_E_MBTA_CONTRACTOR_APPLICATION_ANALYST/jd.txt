MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

Bachelor's degree from an accredited institution.

Three (3) years of experience in system analysis, including enterprise
application design, configuration/development, implementation, and
support.

Substitutions:
A high school diploma or GED with an additional seven (7) years of
directly related experience substitutes for the bachelor's degree
requirement.
An associate's degree from an accredited institution and an additional
three (3) years of directly related experience substitutes for the
bachelor's degree requirement.
A master's degree in a related subject substitutes for two (2) years of
general experience.
A nationally recognized certification, or statewide/professional
certification in a related field substitutes for one (1) year of
experience.

Ability to map and document complex business processes.

Experience identifying and documenting business rules and technical
requirements.

Foundational knowledge of cloud-based application delivery models such as
Software as a Service (SaaS).

Experience with IT service management/ticketing systems.

Strong interpersonal and communication skills.

Microsoft Office suite proficiency.
